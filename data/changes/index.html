Edits to the ten judgments

Each page shows the Greek text given to the models, with every difference from the judgment as published marked. Removed names and passages are withheld.

How to read this page. The text is the material given to the models, in Greek, exactly as published in data/stimuli/gr/. Every place where it differs from the judgment as published on CyLaw is marked. replaced follows a placeholder that stands for a removed name, place or number; the removed text is withheld. removed marks a passage taken out, with its length in words; the passage is withheld. Struck-through text followed by underlined text is a change of wording, most often a corrected misspelling or corrupted character; where the original could identify the case it is withheld. Unmarked text is unchanged. The page ends at the cut: the rest of the judgment, containing the court's reasoning and order, is not published.
