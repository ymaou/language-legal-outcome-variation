1. By the present Application, the Claimant/Applicant (hereinafter it will be referred to as the "Claimant") seeks the issue of summary judgment against the Defendant/Respondent (hereinafter it will be referred to as the "Defendant"), as per points I, III, V, VII and VIII of paragraph 21 of the Statement of Claim which accompanies its Claim Form.





2. Specifically, the Claimant seeks judgment and /or an order of the Court for the eviction of the Defendant from the shop on ΧΧΧΧΧ street, [location], [location] with registration No. ΧΧΧΧΧ, in the Parish  [location] of [name of municipality] (hereinafter the "Disputed Shop"), for intermediate benefits and/or profits and damages in the amount of €700 monthly from the date of filing of the Claim until the vacating and delivery of vacant possession of the premises to the Claimant, for the payment of any common expenses in arrears and/or water supply charges and/or telephone charges and/or other Municipal and/or State charges and/or taxes connected with the use of the premises until the vacating and delivery of vacant possession of the premises to the Claimant, costs and legal interest.





II. SUMMARY OF THE PLEADED POSITIONS OF THE PARTIES


THE CLAIM OF THE CLAIMANT


3. In the statement of claim which accompanies the Claim Form of the Claimant, the latter alleges that from 17/8/2011 and/or at all times material to its claim it is the owner and/or beneficiary of the disputed shop, which does not fall within the provisions of the Rent Control Law but lies within the district of [location] and consequently within the jurisdiction of the present Court.





4. The Defendant is a law firm and by virtue of a written tenancy agreement dated 1/6/2016, rented the disputed shop from the Claimant for the period 1/6/2016 until 31/12/2023 for a monthly rent which was agreed at the sum of €600, payable on the 1st of each month, with a grace period of 10 days and/or an increase by 5% every two years and with a right of extension of the tenancy on the part of the Defendant by the sending of written notice at least 2 months before its expiry.





5. According to the allegations of the Claimant, the parties signed a new tenancy agreement for the disputed shop on or about 1/6/2020, which provided for a tenancy period from 1/6/2020 until 31/5/2022, monthly rent of €660 and a right of extension of the tenancy period on the part of the Defendant by the sending of written notice at least 2 months before its expiry and/or it lay with the Claimants whether they would accept the said extension. The Claimant alleges that the aforementioned agreement is void and/or of no effect inasmuch as it was not signed in the presence and/or does not bear the signatures of 2 witnesses.





6. The sum of the rent which the Defendant paid was subsequently increased to the sum of €700 and/or despite the protests of the Claimants its payment was made with repeated delays and/or omissions.





7. It was an express and/or implied term of the tenancy agreement and/or agreements that the Defendants would pay, beyond the rent, also all the taxes and charges which were imposed in relation to the use and/or possession of the disputed shop.





8. On or about 5/5/2023 the Claimant, through an advocate, by letter called upon the Defendant, on account of the expiry of the tenancy agreement, to deliver vacant and free possession of the disputed shop by 31/12/2023, to which the latter failed to respond. On or about the the 28/9/2023, the Claimant sent a new letter manifesting its intention not to renew the tenancy agreement of the disputed shop and calling upon the Defendant anew to deliver the possession of the latter within the same time limit. The Defendant replied to the last letter of the Claimant by the letter dated 14/12/2023 refusing to comply and the Claimant called upon it anew in writing on 18/12/2023 to respect what had been agreed and to deliver the disputed shop within the time limit specified on its part.





9. Because of the non-compliance of the Defendant with the content of the above mentioned letters, on 30/5/2024 the Claimant sent to the Defendant a letter by which it terminated the tenancy from month to month and/or otherwise, called upon it to deliver possession of the disputed apartment by 30/6/2024 and informed it that any payment by the Defendant would be received as damages on account of the unlawful trespass and with full reservation of the rights of the Claimant.





10. The Defendant by its letter dated 5/6/2024 stated that it was not going to comply with the demand of the Claimant and until today it has not delivered the possession of the disputed one to the Claimant and has become an unlawful trespasser with the result that the Claimant is deprived of enjoyment and/or enjoyment of its property and suffers a loss on a monthly basis until the return of the possession of the immovable property in the amount of €700 and/or equal to the rental value, about €1.500.





11. Further and/or alternatively the Claimant alleges that the Defendant by its conduct has accepted the termination of the tenancy since it fails to pay all the monthly sums which it ought to pay.








DEFENCE


12. The Defendant appeared in the procedure by filing through an advocate a notice of appearance on 8/11/2024 noting on the relevant form that it intends to dispute the claim or part of it, as well as the jurisdiction of the Court.





13. Subsequently, the Defendant filed a defence through which it raises preliminary objections in relation to a) the lack of standing of the Claimant to promote the present claim against it, b) the non-compliance of the Claimant with the content of the Protocol which it sent, and c) the non-disclosure of a reasonable cause for bringing a claim in the pleading of the Claimant and the abusiveness of it, at the same time seeking the striking out of it on the basis of Part 3 of the New Civil Procedure Rules.





14. In relation to the substance of the claim, the Defendant admits in its defence the capacity of the Claimant in relation to the disputed apartment and that it itself constitutes a law firm with a registered office in [location] but is ignorant of and consequently rejects the majority of the remaining allegations of the Claimant.





15. In relation to the meaning and/or force and/or validity and/or the lawfulness of the terms of the agreements at issue, it reserves its right to refer more extensively at the hearing. In relation to the allegations of the Claimant in relation to the jurisdiction of the present Court, the Defendant alleges that the disputed shop falls within the Rent Control Law.


III. APPLICATION UNDER CONSIDERATION & OPPOSITION


APPLICATION


16. The present application was filed after the completion of the pleadings in the claim and is founded, among others, upon Part 23 rules 1,2,4, 4(6), 5, 11, 12 and 13 and Part 24 rules 1-6(1)(a) of the Civil Procedure Rules and is supported by an Affidavit of the director, secretary and sole shareholder of the Claimant company (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-ΣΓ").


17. The deponent within the framework of the ΕΔ-ΣΓ repeats mainly, to a more extensive degree however and in greater detail, the pleaded positions of the Claimant, as they emerge from the Statement of Claim, attaching relevant documents as exhibits in support of these as follows: Copy of the identity card of the deponent ( Exhibit 1), printed result of a search from the official website of the Registrar of Companies for the Claimant in which there appears the capacity of the deponent in relation to the latter ( Exhibit 2), copy of the title of ownership of the disputed apartment in the name of the Claimant ( Exhibit 3), printed result of a search from the official website-portal of the Land Registry and Surveys in which there appears the value of the premises for 2021 ( Exhibit 4), copy of a written tenancy agreement dated 1/6/2016 ( Exhibit 5), copy of a written tenancy agreement dated 1/6/2020 ( Exhibit 6) and correspondence exchanged between the parties ( Exhibit 7-11).


18. Further, in relation to the jurisdiction of the present Court, he states that the Claimant was registered as owner and took possession of the disputed shop in 2011 and as a result has no reason whatsoever to believe nor does it know whether it was under or for rent on 31/12/1999, as per the relevant criterion of the Law.


19. In addition, through the ΕΔ-ΣΓ, the deponent limits, for the reasons which he states, the claim of the Claimant for damages to the last agreed rent, that is €700 monthly from the filing of the claim until the delivery of the disputed shop and declares that the Defendant has no real prospect of successful defence of the claim and he knows of no other reason for which the claim ought to be tried.


OPPOSITION


20. The Defendant filed an opposition, which is based, among others, upon Part 8 rule 7 and Part 24 of the Civil Procedure Rules.





21. By the opposition of the Defendant there are advanced various grounds for which the application of the Claimant ought not to succeed and which I set out summarily as follows: 1. The preconditions of the Rules for the issue of summary judgment against the Defendant are not satisfied, 2. The Application is substantively and/or legally unfounded, 3. A cause of action is not confirmed from the content of the ΕΔ-ΣΓ and the latter does not contain the necessary facts for the establishment of the claim, 4. The Defendant has a bona fide and well-founded Defence and has revealed such facts as give it the right to promote it and does not aim at the delay of the case, 5. The exhibits attached to the ΕΔ-ΣΓ are not legally and substantively sufficient for the issue of summary judgment since most of them are not originals and sufficient reasoning is not given for the production of copies and/or the best possible evidence was not adduced, 6. Legal points are raised which will have to be examined after the required evidence is given and which, if they are accepted, will demonstrate that the claim of the Claimant does not stand, 7. The claim of the Claimant is not liquidated, and 8. The Application was filed late, in bad faith, abusively and oppressively, with the sole purpose of depriving the Defendant of the possibility of defending its position.


22. The facts upon which the opposition is based emerge from the affidavit of the sole director and lawful representative of the Defendant (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-ΖΦ") who in support of its grounds of opposition, states that the disputed shop falls within the controlled areas which are governed by the Rent Control Law, that the Defendant ought to have carried out a thorough investigation in relation to its use on 31/12/1999 and that the said premises were erected and configured with the exclusive purpose of tenancy.


23. The deponent confirms the tenancy of the disputed shop through a written agreement on 1/6/2016 and states that since then her business operates continuously and without interruption in the said premises for eight (8) consecutive years, having developed a stable clientele, commercial reputation and recognisability in the field of the services provided.


24. According to the deponent, on account of the long-lasting and uninterrupted tenancy relationship between the parties and the acceptance of increases on the part of the Defendant, there existed a reasonable and legitimate expectation that the lease would continue without the sending of written notice on behalf of the Defendant being required and that it would be renewed tacitly. More particularly, she states that the owner himself never raised such an issue, but on the contrary on 1/6/2020 he proposed the signing of a new written agreement until 31/5/2022, with the purpose of an increase of rent, to which it consented. The said agreement expired on 1/6/2022 and subsequently was renewed from month to month and it possesses the disputed shop up to and including today paying the sum of €700 monthly. From 1/6/2022 up to and including 5/5/2023 it used the premises without any complaint or objection on behalf of the Claimant, indeed on or about 1/1/2023, the latter proceeded to a new increase of the rent raising it to the sum of €700, which it both accepted and pays unfailingly.


25. In relation to the tenancy agreement dated 1/6/2020, the deponent states that it is not void, given that it bears correctly completed the relevant attestations, as is required by the Law and, in proof of her position, she attaches the said agreement as Exhibit A. Further she states that the agreement was being performed by both parties and the Claimant accepted the agreed rent without any objection or reservation.





26. It is also stated in the ΕΔ-ΖΦ that the Defendant does not owe any sum to the Authorities and makes the necessary payments regularly and in good time on the basis of what was agreed. The deponent further recounts the history of the communication and correspondence between the parties after the letter of the Claimant dated 5/5/2023 and explains that the real purpose of the eviction sought is because it did not agree to a new increase of the rent to the sum of €1.800.


SUPPLEMENTARY WRITTEN EVIDENCE BY THE CLAIMANT


27. Supplementary to the ΕΔ-ΣΓ there was also filed the Affidavit by an advocate of the law firm which represents the Claimant (hereinafter the "ΣΕΔ-ΜΙ"), in which are stated the reasons for which the deponent of the ΕΔ-ΣΓ does not make the affidavit and the source of information of the deponent as to the facts of the case under consideration.





28. In relation to the non-filing of original documents, the deponent explains the reason why copies of Exhibits 1, 2 and 6 but also of the various letters and confirmations of their dispatch were attached. More particularly in relation to Exhibit 6 ΕΔ-ΣΓ, she states that the Claimant did not manage to locate the original agreement and out of necessity the copy of it was filed. The original agreement was filed as Exhibit A ΕΔ-ΖΦ where it appears that it is signed by two witnesses, nevertheless the copy which the Claimant has in its possession does not bear the signatures of witnesses. In any event however, she states that the said agreement, as is also admitted by the Defendant, has expired and does not affect the substance of the Claim of the Claimant.





29. In rebuttal of the relevant positions which are advanced in the ΕΔ-ΖΦ, the deponent refers to delays in the payment of the rents by the Defendant after 17/3/2025 and attaches as Exhibit 1 copies of screenshots where the relevant payments appear.


SUPPLEMENTARY WRITTEN EVIDENCE BY THE DEFENDANT


30. Supplementary to the ΕΔ-ΖΦ there was also filed the Affidavit (hereinafter the "ΣΕΔ-ΖΦ") by the same deponent, in which she rejects the positions which are advanced in the ΣΕΔ-ΜΙ raises an issue as to the filing of the latter by a third person. She states further that the non-filing of original documents undermines the possibility of the Defendant to confirm the genuineness and completeness of them, that the delays in the payments of rents were not abusive but an indication of good will and reflected efforts at settlement of the differences of the parties out of court and she maintains her position as to the validity of the agreement dated 1/6/2020.
