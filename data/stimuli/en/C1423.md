By its claim the Claimant seeks the issue of judgment in its favour and against the Defendants as follows:





"A) An Order of the Court ordering Defendants 1, 2 and 3 personally and/or through their agents and/or employees to cease trespassing on the immovable property and/or building owned by the Claimants.





B) An Order of the Court ordering Defendants 1, 2and 3 personally and/or through their agents and/or employees to vacate the immovable property and/or building owned by the Claimants.and immediately deliver to the Claimants free possession and use of them.





C) €11.344,63 plus legal interest on account of rents owed until 03/06/25 or indeed 31/07/25 and thereafter until today or indeed generally as damages and/or as intermediate profits of the Defendant to the detriment of the Claimants and/or as lost revenues and/or by virtue of unjust enrichment and/or by virtue of unlawful trespass or indeed otherwise.





D) Daily the sum of €726.88 plus legal interest from 04/06/25 or indeed from 31/07/25 or indeed from the date of filing of the present action up to and including the day of delivery and/or vacating of the said premises and/or building and/or the said sum as damages and/or as intermediate profits of Defendant 1 under the guarantee of Defendants 2 and 3 to the detriment of the Claimants and/or lost revenues and/or by virtue of unjust enrichment and/or by virtue of unlawful trespass or indeed otherwise.





E) A declaratory declaration that the tenancy agreement dated 31/12/17 has been terminated on 03/06/25.





F) Costs plus VAT."





The Defendants filed an appearance on 2.10.2025 and up to today have not filed a defence.





The Claimant (hereinafter "the Applicant") proceeded to file the application under consideration on 6.10.2025 for the issue of summary judgment against the Defendants (hereinafter the "Respondents").



The Application


The application is accompanied by an affidavit of Α.Κ. who states that he is one of the two directors of the Applicant and knows the facts from his personal involvement. The Applicant is the owner of three plots within which a three-storey building has been erected. For a certain period of time, as he states, within the building a business operated and it was rented by Respondent 1 with Respondents 2 and 3 being its guarantors, by virtue of a written tenancy agreement dated 13.12.2017 (Exhibit 1) which was signed between the Respondents and the previous owner.





On or about 23.5.2023 the company [name of previous owner company] (hereinafter "[name of previous owner company]") succeeded the previous owner and subsequently the Applicant became the owner by virtue of an agreement dated 26.6.2025. The Applicant was registered as owner on 18.7.2025 (Exhibit 2). In the meantime the tenancy continued and Respondent 1 had accepted the transfer.





According to Exhibit 1, Respondent 1 was obliged to pay rent in the amount of €6.500 payable on the 1st day of each month from 1.3.2018. There would be an increase of the order of 5% every two years in the rent. Respondent 1 was obliged to pay the rent unfailingly without delay and in the event that it delayed in paying the rent on the date on which it became payable, there existed the right of termination on the part of the owner following written notice and a demand that the building be vacated immediately and free and vacant possession of it be delivered, payment of all the rents in arrears, payment of outstanding bills, damages and other expenses. In the case where there would be no compliance Respondent 1 would pay damages for each day that it remained in the building.





Respondent 1 retained the right to deliver in writing, two months before the expiry of the tenancy, notice concerning its intention to renew the tenancy from 1.3.2021 until 28.2.2026 and thereafter from 1.3.2026 until 28.2.2031. It was a necessary precondition for the renewal the prior faithful observance on the part of Respondent 1 of all the terms of the contract (Exhibit 1). Respondents 2 and 3 who are director and secretary respectively of the Respondent guaranteed the faithful observance of all the terms of Exhibit 1 by Respondent 1.





As Α.Κ. further states in the affidavit which accompanies the application, Respondent 1 did not pay any rent whatsoever between 23.5.2023 and 3.6.2025. As a result, [name of previous owner company], by a letter through an advocate dated 3.6.2025 (Exhibit 3) which it sent to the Respondents informed them, among others, of the existence of a debt in the amount of €46.902,06 which concerned the rents between June 2023 and June 2025, terminated the tenancy contract and that the above debt would increase for each day that it remained within the building. It also called upon the Respondents to, among others, remove every trespass on the building and deliver free and vacant possession by 31.7.2025 and pay the above sum.





By a letter of 1.7.2025 Respondent 1 denied the above (Exhibit 4). Despite the said letter of it, Respondent 1 paid various sums on account of its above debt, admitting according to Α.Κ. that there had been a breach of the terms of the contract (Exhibit 1) and that the above contract was lawfully terminated with the result that the sum of €11.344,63 remained owing. However it did not comply and did not respond to the rest of what was demanded by the letter Exhibit 3 above.





The letter of 1.7.2025 was answered by [name of previous owner company] by a letter of 17.7.2025 (Exhibit 5) by which it confirmed the termination of the contract (Exhibit 1) and stated among others, that the above sum remained owing for the rents 23.5.2023 to 30.6.2025 the payment of which it demanded, and called upon Respondents 1 anew to deliver free and vacant possession of the building by 31.7.2025.





The contract of sale and purchase of the building (Exhibit 9) according to the deponent provided that the tenancy contract (Exhibit 1) had been terminated through the letter Exhibit 3 above and that all the rights of the previous owner which derive from Exhibit 1, were assigned to the Applicant from the date of transfer, including the rights of collection of any rents owed before or after the transfer, the right of eviction as well as all the rights which the previous owner held against Respondent 1.





The Applicant, after it was registered as owner, by its letter dated 21.7.2025 (Exhibit 6) informed the Respondents that it has become the lawful owner of the building, that all the rights and obligations of [name of previous owner company] have been assigned to it from the date of registration of the building in the name of the Applicant and that the letter Exhibit 3 is equivalent to a letter sent by the Applicant. It further demanded that free and vacant possession of the building be delivered by 31.7.2025 and the payment of damages for each day from 4.6.2025 for unlawful trespass.





The Respondents, according to Α.Κ. by a letter dated 23.7.2025 (Exhibit 7) advanced various baseless allegations and any positions or allegations of the latter in relation to additions and works which have raised the value of the building or claims of theirs in that regard, have no connection whatsoever with the dispute between them but between the Respondents and [name of previous owner company]. The Applicant replied by a letter of the same date (Exhibit 8).





The deponent states in addition that up to today Respondent 1 continues to be within the building and to carry on business activity without paying any rent whatsoever and from what he understands the Respondents derive profits on account of their unlawful stay in the place. He further maintains that according to a relevant valuation (Exhibit 10) Respondent 1 must from 4.6.2025 or from 1.8.2025 pay to the Applicant the sum of €726,88 daily on account of its unlawful trespass on the building, until the delivery of the immovable property. The above sum it must pay to the Applicant as damages, intermediate profits of Respondent 1 to the detriment of the Applicant or by virtue of unjust enrichment or unlawful trespass. It is also his position that the purchase value of the immovable property amounts to €3.425.000 and the rental value to €726,88 daily and that the Applicant had paid a sum of almost €2.000.000 for the purchase of the building which it cannot exploit as it wishes on account of the unlawful trespass on the part of Respondent 1. Alternatively, he notes, that the tenancy contract has been terminated automatically on account of the non-payment of rents on the part of Respondent 1.


Finally he states that from what he is advised by the advocates of the Applicant, the Respondents have absolutely no real prospect of success whatsoever in any defence and he does not know of any reason for which the claim ought to be tried. And he proceeds in conclusion to state, among others, that the claim of the Applicant is documented while he characterises as hopeless the defence of the Respondents because, there was a clear breach of the tenancy contract, the tenancy contract was lawfully terminated, the Respondent is trespassing unlawfully on the building since it continues to be within it without paying rent from 4.6.2025 or 1.8.2025, Respondents 2 and 3 personally guaranteed Respondent 1 and the breach was acknowledged on the part of the Respondent hence it proceeded to payments. The facts according to the deponent are without dispute and the Court can easily ascertain that there is no prospect of success of any defence.





The Opposition


By their opposition the Respondents advance 14 grounds for which, according to them the application under consideration cannot succeed. Because among the grounds of opposition preliminary issues are advanced I set them out verbatim immediately below:





"1. The Respondents raise a first preliminary objection and allege that, the Applicant is not entitled to promote the present action/application since it never contracted with the Respondents, and the Applicant is a possessor, and/or tenant, and/or statutory tenant, and/or possessor of the immovable property at issue in accordance with an order of the Court in the action of the District Court of [location] with No. [number of previous action] and of the order issued dated 24/05/21 and/or it was an obligation of the applicant to take notice of the order issued.





2. The Respondents raise a second preliminary objection and allege that the District Court of [location] lacks jurisdiction to deal with the action under the above number and title, inasmuch as the immovable property at issue is situated in the Municipality of [location], which falls within the areas controlled under rent control on the basis of ΚΔΠ 519/2007 as amended by ΚΔΠ 232/2024 and at the time at issue it was rented by the company and/or by the owners of the company [name of previous landlord company].





3. The Respondents raise a third preliminary objection and allege that, the Applicant is not entitled to promote the present action/application since it never contracted with the Respondents, never sent them a letter of termination since the Respondents contracted with [name of previous landlord company].





4. The Respondents raise a fourth preliminary objection and allege that, the Applicant is not entitled to promote the present action/application since it never contracted with the Respondents, never sent them a letter of termination since the Respondents continued to pay rents to [name of previous landlord company] as per the Order of the District Court of [location] in action No. [number of previous action] dated 24/06/2021 which issued an ancillary order which bound the Respondents specifying an account into which the rents ought to be paid.





5. The Respondents raise a fifth preliminary objection and allege that, the Applicant is not entitled to promote the present action/application since it never contracted with the Respondents, never sent them a letter of termination since the Respondents continued to pay rents to [name of previous owner company] after they were informed by documents that the immovable property was transferred into their name and always in obedience to the Order of the District Court of [location] in action No. [number of previous action] dated 24/06/2021.





6. Alternatively and/or without prejudice to the above preliminary objections, the Respondents deny the claim of the Claimant company and raise further grounds of opposition as follows.





7. The application is legally and substantively unfounded.





8. The Applicant does not set out all the actual facts, while it knows them and/or is obliged to place them before the Court it does not provide the necessary facts for the establishment of the claim and/or does not disclose material facts.





9. The affidavit which accompanies the application of the Applicant company and the attached exhibits are not legally and substantively sufficient for the establishment of the claim and/or the issue of summary judgment.





10. The Respondents have a very good, substantial and bona fide defence and counterclaim in the present action. The facts of the case which the defendants set out in the affidavit which accompanies the present opposition constitute a plausible or arguable defence as well as a counterclaim and show that the Respondents - Defendants must be permitted to raise their defence.





11. The Respondents have a good defence on the substance, which is bona fide and on legal matters and appears in the affidavit of the director [name of respondents' director] and in no case is it for purposes of delay of the case that the notice of appearance has been filed.





12. The Respondents - Defendants deny that they owe the sum demanded and raise a counterclaim for a sum which the Applicant company owes to the Respondents, as he explains in the affidavit of the director, [name of respondents' director].





13. The present application was filed abusively, oppressively, maliciously and with the sole purpose of depriving the Respondents of defending their position on the basis of article 30 of the Constitution and the ECHR.





14. The preconditions which Part 24 Rule 2 of the Civil Procedure Rules of 2023 sets for the issue of summary judgment do not exist "





The opposition is accompanied by an affidavit of Respondent 2 and in it he repeats in substance the preliminary objections which are raised by the opposition as they are recorded above. In addition, he alleges, among others, that the Respondents have a very good, substantial and bona fide defence and the facts which he invokes show that there is a plausible or arguable defence as well as a counterclaim. He also alleges that the Respondents do not owe any sum whatsoever to the Applicant but it is the Applicant who owes them money which they counterclaim and he charges the Applicant with not having set out the facts completely before the Court.





Respondent 2 proceeds subsequently to allege that in the application under consideration it has not been demonstrated by the deponent for the Applicant that he is a person who has positive knowledge of the facts but only that his knowledge emerges through information which he has drawn from other persons and documents and he makes the affidavit formally while he has no connection with the present case.





Always according to Respondent 2, the Respondents have been within the building since December of 2017 and never took notice of or consented to the transfer but only when a letter of the Applicant dated 21.7.2025 was notified to them. He admits that Respondent 1 rented the building from [name of previous landlord company] by virtue of a tenancy contract dated 13.12.2017 (Exhibit 1 of the application) with the personal guarantee of himself and of Respondent 3, he does not accept however that the building constituted for a certain period a lawful place for the carrying on of business and he advances the position that the Respondents possessed it, possess it and rent it lawfully. And in relation to the terms of Exhibit 1 of the application, Respondent 2 alleges that it never constituted a precondition in accordance with the tenancy contract that its renewal depended on the observance of all its terms, material and not. The Respondents observed devoutly the terms of the said contract and sent a letter for its renewal on 14.5.2025 something which took place within the prescribed time limit and was on the basis of the contract satisfactory for purposes of renewal.





The side of the Respondents also advances the allegation that they do not owe any sum whatsoever to the Applicant inasmuch as they pay or indeed paid rents as they had been ordered to do by virtue of an order of the D.C. [location] dated 24.6.2021 (Exhibit 2) and at the material time they were paying rents to [name of previous owner company] into an account which had been indicated to them and these were paid until July of 2025 (Exhibit 3). There is an admission of the sending of a pre-action protocol, Respondent 2 however alleges that what is stated there are baseless allegations while he alleges in addition that the only reason for which the Respondents received a letter of termination / pre-action protocol is because they exercised their right to renew the tenancy contract by the letter of their advocates above. He admits however the receipt of a letter dated 17.7.2025 (Exhibit 5 of the application) and the letter dated 21.7.2025 (Exhibit 6 of the application) despite the fact that the latter was served on a person unconnected with Respondent 1.





It constitutes an additional allegation that the sums which the Applicant claims have not been explained in any manner whatsoever and are not supported by any exhibit whatsoever. The Applicant, since July of 2025 when it became owner, attempts maliciously and in bad faith to extract money from the Respondents presenting it as VAT or charges or increases which had in no way been agreed in the contract. After July of 2025 no rent whatsoever was paid inasmuch as with the change of the ownership status the advocates of the Applicant refused to notify the account details of the Applicant for payment of the rents despite the fact that it was demanded by the Respondents by a letter of their advocates (Exhibit 5).





Respondent 2 further advances the position that it was an express and/or tacit agreement and/or consent between Respondent 1 and [name of previous landlord company] when they were signing the tenancy contract that the Respondents would be permitted to carry out improvement works so that it would be converted into a suitable place for the carrying on of business. This had as a result that the value of the immovable property increased and according to a valuation (Exhibit 4) the damage which the Respondents will suffer in the event of eviction will amount to a sum of €1.500.000,00 and they have the intention to claim or demand by counterclaim general damages from the Applicant for damage to their reputation and/or clientele on account of the conduct of the Applicant. And according to the Respondent, [name of previous owner company] tricked or indeed deceived the Applicant by assuring it that the tenancy was terminated so that the impression would be created in the Applicant that it would receive an immovable property with vacant possession but with the additions which the Respondents made who continue to possess the immovable property lawfully on the basis of the tenancy contract. The Applicant ought to have moved legally against [name of previous owner company] and not against the Respondents.





It is in conclusion his position that on the basis of what the Respondents raise, the case is not suitable to be tried summarily but the matters must be decided within the framework of a normal trial. From what he is advised by the advocates of the Respondents the latter have a very good defence and very good chances of success in their counterclaim and for them not to be heard at a hearing would be against the interest of justice.














Supplementary Affidavit of the Applicant


By a supplementary affidavit of [name of applicant's director], director of the Applicant, the latter replies, among others, to the preliminary objections of the Respondents. He alleges among others that on the basis of the order of the D.C. [location] a statutory tenancy is not recognised but, that interim order ordered [name of previous landlord company] not to alienate the sums which it received from rents of the Respondents and not to disclose information. The said order became devoid of object since [name of previous owner company] became the new owner. He points out further, that in the form of appearance which the Respondents filed they did not select the dispute of jurisdiction but only the dispute of the claim with the result that they are prevented from raising issues of jurisdiction. In any event the building permit for the building at issue with number [building permit number] was issued in 2006 and the building was erected in 2007. Consequently it cannot fall within the jurisdiction of rent control since it did not exist in 1999. And he proceeds to repeat the positions which were advanced in the original affidavit, he points out contradictions in the evidence of the Respondents and maintains the lawfulness of the termination of the tenancy contract.





Supplementary Affidavit of the Respondents


By a supplementary affidavit of Respondent 2, the Respondents reject, among others, what is recorded in the supplementary affidavit [name of applicant's director] above. There is also added the allegation that on or about the months March - June 2024 [name of previous owner company] had issued a credit note in favour of the Respondents inasmuch as sums greater than the agreed rent had been paid (Exhibit 1 of the supplementary affidavit). In any event it is his position that, the Applicant has not explained how the difference emerges from the sums which [name of previous owner company] claimed with the sum of about Euro 11.000 which the Applicant claims and that the latter does not prove the sum which it claims. He repeats his allegations of the existence of a good defence and of a probability of success in a counterclaim.
