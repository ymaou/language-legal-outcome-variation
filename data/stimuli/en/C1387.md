The Claimant/Applicant (hereinafter he will be referred to as the "Claimant") seeks the issue of summary judgment against the Defendant/Respondent (hereinafter she will be referred to as the "Defendant"), as per the remedies sought under numbers 1,3 and 4 under point D of the Statement of Claim.





2. Specifically, the Claimant seeks judgment and /or an order of the Court for the eviction of the Defendant and/or of any dependant and/or agent of hers and/or of any person who possesses the apartment (hereinafter the "Disputed Apartment"), any further or other remedy which the Court may deem just and reasonable in the circumstances and the costs of the application.



II. THE PLEADED POSITIONS OF THE PARTIES


THE CLAIM OF THE CLAIMANT


3. In the statement of claim which accompanies the Claim Form of the Claimant, the latter alleges that he is a citizen of Russia and owner of the disputed apartment which he purchased through an auction on or about 12/12/23 with the purpose of using it as a permanent or holiday residence himself and his dependants. Specifically, on the basis of what he himself alleges, there was granted to the Claimant a permit of acquisition in relation to the disputed apartment on the condition that it will be used by the Claimant only as a holiday or permanent residence and that no other right whatsoever or for any other purpose is granted.





4. According to the content of the Statement of Claim, the Claimant discovered the existence of a tenant within the disputed apartment only after he visited it with the purpose of changing the locks. The Defendant who is a citizen of the Republic of Cyprus and a permanent resident of Cyprus, rented the disputed apartment from its previous owner on or about 1/3/2019 for 2 years, for a very low monthly rent in the amount of €50, on account of the fact that the owner was her father. The tenancy expired on 28/2/2021 and since then the tenancy expires from the last day of the month which follows. The disputed apartment is used by the Defendant for purposes of residence of herself and/or of her dependants, even after the expiry of the contract.





5. The Claimant alleges that his advocates, through the advocate of the Defendant, asked the latter to deliver to the Claimant the free possession of the disputed apartment. The Defendant however responded by asking for the details of the bank account of the Claimant for the continuation on her part of the payment of the rents, which however he refused to supply to her in view of the above mentioned condition.





6. Subsequently through his advocates on 1/3/2024 he sent a letter to the Defendant with a private process server for the termination of the tenancy from 30/4/2024, a date on which the Claimant considers the tenancy expired, and asking for the delivery of the free possession of the disputed apartment. Because, according to the Statement of Claim, the Defendant was avoiding the process server, the same letter was sent to her on 27/3/2024 double registered through the post and on 29/3/2024 by facsimile and electronic message to her advocate. The Defendant replied to the letter of the Claimant by the sending of a letter of reply and a cheque in the value of €250 for rents owed until May 2024, which was never cashed by the Claimant since he did not accept the continuation of the tenancy.





7. The Claimant alleges that the Defendant refuses to deliver possession of the disputed immovable property on the pretext that she has become a statutory tenant and insists on the tenancy of the disputed apartment despite the aforementioned decision of the Ministry of the Interior. It also constitutes an allegation of the Claimant that the disputed apartment was erected before 1999 but was not rented out or offered for rent until 2000, something which the first page of the tenancy document confirms.


DEFENCE


8. The Defendant appeared in the procedure by filing through an advocate a notice of appearance noting on the relevant form that she intends to dispute the claim or part of it, as well as the jurisdiction of the Court.





9. Subsequently, the Defendant filed a defence through which she raises a preliminary objection as to the competence by subject matter of the present Court to try the present case in view of the fact that the disputed apartment is situated in an area in [location] to which the Rent Control Law applies, was built before 1999, was offered for rent at that time and the Defendant is a statutory tenant of it and a Cypriot citizen, by virtue of a tenancy document. As a result the position of the Defendant is that the competent Court is that of Rent Control [location].





10. The Defendant denies the allegation of the Claimant that the disputed apartment was not offered for rent until 2000 and alleges that the tenancy document which the Claimant invokes concerned two (2) apartments and the said reference on the document corresponded to the second apartment (number 1) and not the disputed apartment (number 2).





11. The Defendant admits that the disputed apartment is used by her with the purpose of her residence and that she remained in it after the expiry of the tenancy contract. She alleges that she paid a low rent for the reasons which are stated in the tenancy agreement and on account of unemployment, underemployment and on account of the period when everything was operating at a reduced level on account of the pandemic.





12. The Defendant denies that the Claimant did not know of the existence of the tenant in the disputed apartment and that he also made a statement, within the framework of a meeting which he had with the management of the apartment building, that it does not bother him provided that the rent is paid. In addition, she denies that she was avoiding the process server of the Claimant.





13. The Defendant alleges that the Claimant is active in Cyprus with purchases and sales of immovable property and purchased the disputed apartment in order to exploit it for purposes other than those for which the permit of the Ministry was secured, misleading the latter. Further, she alleges that the Claimant resides in a dwelling of his own in [location] and moreover the disputed apartment cannot constitute a holiday residence since it is situated in an inhabited area, in the centre of [location] and near the house where the Claimant already resides.





14. It constitutes the position of the Defendant that she resides lawfully in the disputed apartment since she has become a statutory tenant, that the termination by the Claimant is unlawful and legally unfounded, and that she has already paid a number of rents to the Claimant through his advocate and as to that she admits that she asked for the relevant details of the Claimant.


III. APPLICATION UNDER CONSIDERATION & OPPOSITION


APPLICATION


15. The present application is founded, among others, upon Part 24 rules 1-6 of the Civil Procedure Rules and is supported by an Affidavit of the Claimant himself (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-VB").


16. The deponent within the framework of the ΕΔ-VB repeats to a more extensive degree and in greater detail the pleaded positions of the Claimant, as they emerge from the Statement of Claim, attaching relevant documents as exhibits in support of these as follows: Title of ownership and permit of acquisition of it ( Exhibit 1), the tenancy document dated 3/1/2019 between the Defendant and her father ( Exhibit 2), correspondence between the parties/advocates and copies of cheques ( Exhibits 3-7).


17. Further, the deponent requests the issue of summary judgment against the Defendant as per the prayer for relief of the application and states that, as he is advised by his advocates, the Defendant has no real prospect of success on the claim and the legal position upon which his case is based is clear on account of the restriction on the use of the disputed apartment, as it is determined by the Ministry of the Interior, which in no case provides for a right of residence of the Defendant.


OPPOSITION


18. The Defendant filed an opposition, which is based, among others, upon Part 24 rules 1-7 and Part 23 rules 1-8 and 13 of the Civil Procedure Rules.





19. By the opposition of the Defendant there are advanced various grounds for which the application of the Claimant ought not to succeed and which I set out summarily as follows: 1. The preconditions for the issue of summary judgment are not satisfied and the application is irregular and lacks a legal basis, 2. The Defendant advances in her defence a substantial and real bona fide defence which requires the continuation of the procedure for the administration of justice, 3. In the defence a preliminary objection is raised as to the jurisdiction of the Court which cannot be passed over by the examination of the application and facts are revealed which the Defendant must be given the right to advance and there are compelling reasons for which the matter must be decided at a trial, and 4. The Defendant has a real prospect of successful defence of the claim.


20. The facts upon which the opposition is based emerge from the defence of the Defendant and the Affidavit of the Defendant (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-ΑΣ").


21. In the ΕΔ-ΑΣ, the deponent repeats in the form of evidence what is stated in her defence, with emphasis on the position that jurisdiction for the trial of the dispute between them vests in the Rent Control Court since all the relevant preconditions are satisfied and she has become a statutory tenant and consequently, according to her, the Claimant has no standing for her eviction. According to the ΕΔ-ΑΣ, the Claimant deliberately misleads the Court and makes erroneous statements in order to succeed in his claim against her. The deponent states that the disputed apartment was always offered by her father for rent before 2000 and that the reference on the 1st page of the tenancy document upon which the Claimant bases his position concerns another apartment, specifically apartment no.1, while from the 2nd page it appears that the disputed apartment is apartment no.2.


SUPPLEMENTARY WRITTEN EVIDENCE BY THE CLAIMANT (ΣΕΔ-ΜΚ1 and ΣΕΔ-ΜΚ2)


22. Supplementary to the ΕΔ-VB there was also filed the Affidavit by the agent of the Claimant in Cyprus (hereinafter the "ΣΕΔ-ΜΚ1"), in which are stated the reasons for which the Claimant himself does not make the affidavit.


23. The content of the ΣΕΔ-ΜΚ1 in substance answers the allegations which were advanced in the ΕΔ-ΑΣ, with the purpose of rebutting them and by the repetition of the allegations and positions of the Claimant, which were advanced through his evidence. The deponent takes a position on the positions of the Defendant, as they were advanced through the ΕΔ-ΑΣ, and states specifically that the Claimant initially filed an application in the Rent Control Court [location] for the eviction of the Defendant. However in the end he withdrew the application accepting the relevant concerns of the Court as to its jurisdiction. As a result, his side considers that the present Court is the one competent to try the dispute between the parties.





24. The deponent denies that the Claimant made a statement that the presence of a tenant in the disputed apartment does not bother him provided that the rent is paid and states that his intention to evict the Defendant was constant and the cheques of the Defendant are not cashed on his part, which are received with reservation of his rights, and which will be returned to her immediately after any judgment for her eviction.





25. In proof of the above, the deponent presents as exhibits the following documents: Copy of a minute of the Rent Control Court ( Exhibit 1), a letter which confirms the purchase of the disputed apartment at auction ( Exhibit 2) and a letter of the advocate of the Claimant to the Police ( Exhibit 3).





26. Further, supplementary to the ΕΔ-VB and ΣΕΔ-ΜΚ1 there was also filed the ΣΕΔ-ΜΚ2, through which the agent of the Claimant in Cyprus refers to Exhibit 1 of the ΕΔ-VB and specifically as to the erroneous writing on it of the number of the disputed apartment and the consequent correction of it by the competent service. Deponent attaches as exhibits both the erroneous document ( Exhibit 2), as well as the corrected one ( Exhibit 1).
