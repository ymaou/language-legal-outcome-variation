The issue of summary judgment is sought by the Claimant, as per the Claim Form and the Statement of Claim. More particularly:


i. A Declaration and/or Order of the Court that the claimants are entitled immediately to the vacant and free possession of the house which is situated on the street [location](hereinafter referred to as the "house").


ii. An Order of the Court preventing and/or prohibiting the defendant and/or his agents and/or persons who derive a right from him from trespassing in any manner whatsoever and/or possessing and/or entering in any manner whatsoever in the house or from causing the entry and/or trespass of other persons in the house.


iii. An Order of the Court ordering immediately the removal of every trespass of the defendant on the house.


iv. €12.600.- in favour of the claimants and against the defendant as damages and/or as rents owed of the house for the period from 01/10/2022 until 30/06/2024 and/or until 30/06/2024.


v. €600.- monthly as damages and/or as intermediate benefits or profits from 01/07/2024 until the removal of the unlawful trespass of the defendant on the house and/or until the delivery of the free and vacant possession of the house to the claimants.


vi. An Order of the Court ordering the defendant and/or his agents and/or any possessor of the house to deliver immediately to the claimants the vacant and free possession and use of the house


vii. An Order of the Court ordering immediately the eviction of the defendant and/or of any possessor of the house from the house


viii. A Declaration and/or declaratory judgment ( declaratory judgment) that whatever express and/or oral and/or implied tenancy had been or has been granted to the defendant in relation to the house, has been lawfully terminated by the claimants.


ix. Any other remedy which the Court deems just and reasonable in the circumstances.


x. Costs plus VAT plus costs of service.


xi. Legal interest on every sum adjudged from the date of filing of this claim.


PLEADINGS




First of all, I consider it expedient to set out in a summary manner, the content of the pleadings.


According to the Statement of Claim, the Claimant is the registered owner of a house which is situated in [name of municipality] and which was erected after 31.12.1999 (hereinafter the "disputed house").


On or about 31.1.2014, the Claimant rented the disputed house to the Defendant for the period from 1.2.2014 until 31.1.2015 by virtue of a written agreement dated 31.1.2014, with a monthly rent in the amount of €500. Upon the expiry of the tenancy period, the tenancy continued from month to month and following an understanding and/or agreement between the parties, on or about 2018 and/or 2019, the rent was set at €600.





The Defendant up to today possesses the disputed house and paid the rents for the period from 1.2.2014 until 30.9.2022. Since then the Defendant fails and/or refuses to pay any sum whatsoever on account of rent, with the result that the Claimant terminated the tenancy of the house by its letter dated 3.4.2024 and demanded the payment of the rents owed.





By his Defence the Defendant raises preliminary objections according to which (a) the Court is not competent to adjudicate the dispute at issue since the Rent Control Law applies and (b) the action is premature since the tenancy contract was never terminated.





The Defendant denies the allegations of the Claimant and calls upon it to prove them, and he alleges that after the expiry of the tenancy document an oral agreement was concluded between the parties that the rent would be paid yearly, and the tenancy agreement was never terminated, therefore the action is premature.


Further the Defendant alleges that he was always consistent in his obligations, while he carried out repairs to the disputed house which is old and the expenses would be set off against the rents, on the basis of an understanding of his with the Claimant. More particularly, according to an allegation of the Defendant, he paid about the sum of €2.000 for the repair of worn pipes and/or plumbing of the disputed house, a sum which ought to be set off.





Finally, the Defendant alleges that the letter dated 3.4.2024 which he received does not constitute a pre-action protocol on the basis of the Rules since the relevant form is not attached to it and it cannot constitute a lawful termination of the tenancy agreement.





THE POSITIONS OF THE PARTIES WITHIN THE FRAMEWORK OF THE PRESENT PROCEDURE





The background of the facts which support the Application appears in the written evidence of the Director of the Claimant, who, after explaining his capacity, attaching as Exhibit 1 a search from the website of the Registrar of Companies, expresses the belief that his claim, as it is set out in the Claim Form and in the Statement of Claim, is correct and true.





Subsequently the deponent attaches as Exhibit 2 the title of ownership of the disputed house and further states that the Defendant is estopped on account of the contract dated 31.1.2014 from invoking that the Claimant is not the owner of the disputed house, on account of his acts and his conduct, as well as on account of his own admissions in his Defence.





The disputed house, the deponent continues, was erected after 31.12.1999 and consequently the tenancy at issue does not fall within the Rent Control Law. In corroboration of this allegation of his, he attaches as Exhibit 3 the building permit of the disputed house, the number of the relevant file, as well as a copy from the internet portal of the Land Registry in which it is stated that the house was erected in 2006. In any event, the deponent continues, the Defendant has not raised any procedure whatsoever for disputing the jurisdiction of the Court, in accordance with Part 12 of the Civil Procedure Rules.





Subsequently the deponent repeats in substance the allegations of the Statement of Claim and in corroboration of his allegations, he attaches the following exhibits:





- Exhibit 4: Tenancy contract of the disputed house dated 31.1.2014.





- Exhibit 5: Letter dated 3.4.2024.





- Exhibit 6: Electronic message of the Defendant dated 17.4.2024.





- Exhibit 7: Electronic message of the advocates of the Claimant dated 17.4.2025, in reply to the electronic message of the Defendant of the same date.





- Exhibit 8: Receipts of payments of rents from 1.3.2014 until 30.9.2022.





- Exhibit 9: Letter of the Claimant dated 19.2.2019 by which the Defendant was called upon to pay lease payments in the amount of €7.000 for the period 1.1.2018 - 28.2.2019 and by which the tenancy agreement at issue was terminated.





- Exhibit 10: Statement of account of the Claimant where there appear various payments which the Defendant made.





As the deponent also states, it was never agreed that the rent be paid annually, however on 11.9.2019 the Defendant paid 12 rents in arrears with one payment, after he received a relevant letter from the Claimant (Exhibit 9), while on 11.10.2021 he paid rents in arrears of 11 months for the period from 1.2.2021 until 31.12.2021.





Up to today, the deponent continues, the Defendant possesses the disputed house and has not paid to the Claimant any sum whatsoever, and the allegations of unlawful termination do not correspond to reality, since from Exhibit 5 it emerges that all the forms were duly served on the Defendant, while the case under consideration does not concern the application of the Rent Control Law.





Finally, the deponent sets out in the form of a table the allegedly monthly debts of the Defendant, noting that these amount to the sum of €15.000. And in relation to the alleged repairs of the disputed house and the set-off of these expenses against the rents owed, according to the deponent, they constitute afterthoughts of the Defendant, while in any event, no counterclaim has been raised.





The Application ran up against the Opposition of the other side, in which there are set out the following grounds of opposition, which I set out verbatim:





1. The application at issue is unfounded in law and in substance.


2. The application at issue is deficient and non-existent and in any event the issue of summary judgment is not justified.


3. There is a good Defence by the Defendant/Respondent in the action.


4. The Claimants/Applicants have no standing for the bringing of the present application and by extension of the action, inasmuch as the tenancy document at issue was concluded between [name of claimant] and the Defendant.


5. No sum whatsoever is owed by the Defendant/Respondent to the Claimants/Applicants as owed and/or rents in arrears for the period from 01/10/2022 until 30/06/2024 and/or until 30/06/2024.


6. The orders which the Claimants/Applicants seek to be issued are abusive, inasmuch as in the said dwelling the Respondent lives entirely lawfully in accordance with an oral agreement between himself and [name of claimant] that the rent be paid yearly and there is no lawful reason whatsoever for termination of the agreement of the Defendant with [name of claimant]


7. The Defendant/Respondent was never put upon by the Claimant/Applicant to pay any sum of money for the discharge of rents in arrears, inasmuch as rents and/or rents in arrears were not owed before letters of termination were sent.


8. The action under the above number and title is filed so that the Claimants/Applicants may drive the Defendant/Respondent out of the premises at issue and the present application is promoted abusively for the achievement of this purpose.


9. The sums of money which the Claimants/Applicants claim are sought abusively, inasmuch as these are not owed.


10. The present application is made abusively and/or in bad faith by the Claimants/Applicants and/or for the purpose of exercising pressure on the Defendant/Respondent.


11. The application at issue and by extension the action under the above number and title is premature, since there was a later oral agreement between the Defendant/Respondent and [name of claimant], that the rent be paid yearly and/or in any event on account of the fact that various sums of money were paid by the Defendant/Respondent for repairs of the dwelling at issue which would be included in or indeed set off against the agreed rent.


12. The Claimants/Applicants by the present application seek a remedy which is equivalent to the trial of their claim, as this is reflected in the claim form.


13. The Claimants/Applicants have resorted to steps and/or an attempt at abuse of the procedure with an ulterior and/or extraneous purpose to deprive of a fair trial of the present case of the Defendant/Respondent.


14. The premises at issue fall within the provisions of the Rent Control Law 23/1983, inasmuch as they were erected and completed before 31/12/1999. Consequently, the present action lacks legal and substantive weight, is abusive and exposed to dismissal by the respected Court and consequently the same fate ought to be had also by the present application.


15. The orders sought do not serve the interest of justice, but only the extraneous purposes of the Claimants/Applicants and/or the blackmailing purposes of the latter, since they are so drastic against the Defendant/Respondent with the result that his rights are adversely affected.





The facts upon which the Opposition is based appear in the written evidence of the Defendant, in which there are repeated in substance the allegations of the Defendant, as per his Defence.





Further the deponent states that on the basis of the evidence which has been produced, the approval of the Application under consideration is not justified, since no admission whatsoever emerges on his part in relation to the ownership status of the disputed house, and the agreement dated 31.1.2014 was signed between himself and [name of claimant] who presented himself as the owner of the disputed house. As a result, the Claimant has no standing to bring the present action, since it is not a party to the tenancy document and he himself never contracted with it.





The Defendant also states that the Court does not possess jurisdiction to try the case under consideration, since the disputed house is situated within the local boundaries which the Rent Control Law defines and was available for rent before 31.12.1999 and the time of issue of the building permit in no case determines the time when the premises at issue were erected and made available for the first time for rent.





As to the letter dated 3.4.2024, beyond what is stated in his Defence, the Defendant further alleges that the tenancy agreement was not attached to the said letter, a reason for which his side was not able to take a position on the specific agreement, while the Claimant omits to state that by his electronic message to the advocates of the Claimant, he reserved his right to take a position on their positions at a later stage.





The receipts which are attached as Exhibits, the deponent continues, confirm his allegation of yearly payment of the rents and [name of claimant] chose to issue a separate receipt for each month, for reasons of his own.





Finally, the Defendant clarifies that his Defence concerns the fact that on the one hand the rent is paid yearly, and on the other hand that he has paid certain significant sums which concern repairs of the disputed house, which it was agreed would be set off against the sum of the rents.





SUPPLEMENTARY WRITTEN EVIDENCE





The supplementary written evidence which was filed on the part of the Claimant is signed by a Secretary of it, who initially rejects the allegations of the other side.





Further the deponent rejects the allegation that [name of claimant] presented himself as the owner of the disputed house to the Defendant and that the tenancy contract was signed between these two persons, and she points out that in any event these allegations are not pleaded.





The deponent also states that the receipts were always issued in the name of the Claimant, that the Defendant knew that the owner of the disputed house was the Claimant and not [name of claimant] and that the original tenancy contract expired in 2015 and was renewed from month to month, therefore it is irrelevant who signed it originally.





In addition, the deponent states that the dwelling at issue was erected after 31.12.1999, consequently it was not available for rent before the said date.





Attaching as Exhibit 11 the receipts of the period from April of 2022 until September of 2022, which as she explains had inadvertently not been attached to Exhibit 8, the deponent further states that the payment of €1000.- which was made on 31.10.2022 was allocated to the rents owed of February and January of 2022 which were the oldest rents owed, while the allegation of the Defendant that the rents owed ought to begin from March of 2022, constitutes an admission of his debt.





By supplementary written evidence of his, the Defendant denies and rejects the allegations of the Claimant and further states that his allegation in relation to the ownership status of the dwelling at issue, is pleaded in paragraph 4 of his Defence. As indeed emerges from Exhibit 4 which the Claimant attached to the Application under consideration, the person whom the Defendant was paying was [name of claimant] and as a result, he could not know that another person was the owner of the dwelling at issue.





In relation to the allegedly rents owed, the deponent states that the Claimant has presented contradictory versions, since on the one hand it alleges rents owed from March of 2022 and on the other, that payments were made on 31.10.2022 for the months February and January of 2022.
