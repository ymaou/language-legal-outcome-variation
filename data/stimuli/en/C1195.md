The Claimant / Applicant (hereinafter it will be referred to as the "Claimant") seeks the issue of summary judgment against Defendants 1, 2 and 3 / Respondents (hereinafter they will be referred to together as the "Defendants") as per its claim on its claim form. Specifically, it seeks the issue of orders for the removal of the allegedly unlawful trespass on the part of the Defendants on the plot with No.[plot number] which is situated at the location ΧΧΧΧΧΧΧ, in the parish of [location], and which constitutes property of the Claimant (hereinafter it will be referred to as the "Disputed Immovable Property"), €378 monthly as intermediate benefits and/or damages for unlawful trespass from 20/2/24 and/or 4/9/23, plus legal interest, and general and/or special damages for damage and/or losses which the Claimant suffers as a consequence of the unlawful trespass and/or of the reduction of the value of the disputed immovable property as a consequence of damage which it suffered from the Defendants.


II. THE PLEADED POSITIONS OF THE PARTIES


THE CLAIM OF THE CLAIMANT


2. In the statement of claim which accompanies the Claim Form of the Claimant, the latter alleges that it became the registered owner of the disputed immovable property on 4/9/2023 through the procedure of transfer of registration of mortgaged immovable property at the Land Registry by virtue of the relevant provisions of Law 9/1965 in substitution of the Defendants who were its previous registered owners and/or possessors. As a consequence of the aforementioned transfer of registration, the Claimant alleges that the Defendants thereafter became unlawful trespassers on the disputed immovable property.


3. The Claimant, by the sending of relevant letters, informed the Defendants of the registration of the disputed immovable property in its name, at the same time calling upon them to remove their objects and to deliver to it free and vacant possession of it within a specified time limit. In addition, a pre-action protocol was also sent by it to the Defendants. The latter however did not respond to its letters nor did they comply with the content of them and they continue unlawfully to retain and to trespass on the disputed immovable property.


4. It also constitutes an allegation of the Claimant that the rental value of the disputed immovable property at the time of the filing of the claim form was €378 monthly, which sum reflects the damage which it has suffered on a monthly basis from 20/2/24 and/or 4/9/23 and will continue to suffer until the delivery of the possession of the immovable property by the Defendants. Further, according to the Claimant, the market value of the disputed immovable property amounted at the same time to the sum of €227.000.


THE DEFENCE OF THE DEFENDANTS


5. The Defendants appeared in the procedure by filing a joint notice of appearance, noting on the relevant form that they intend to dispute the claim or part of it.





6. Subsequently, the Defendants filed a joint defence through which they raise preliminary objections in relation to the following issues: a) the bringing of the action against a legally non-existent and/or wrong person as regards Defendant 3, b) the abusiveness of the action in view of the parallel promotion of other proceedings for the pursuit of similar purposes, and c) the non-payment of the legal costs of the Defendants in action No.[number of previous action] (hereinafter the "Action [number of previous action]") in accordance with the Order and/or minute of the Court dated 15/1/24.





7. In relation to the substance of the claim, while the Defendants on the one hand deny that the Claimant is the registered owner of the disputed immovable property, they admit the transfer of its registration in the name of the Claimant through Law 9/1965 alleging however that the said procedure suffers from nullity, is unlawful, unconstitutional and violates human rights entrenched by the Law. The Defendants allege that a relevant action has been filed (hereinafter the "Action [number of pending parallel action]") on their part together with a third person against the Claimant and a third company, with the same parties and the same issues in dispute as the present one, which is pending.





8. The Defendants admit that Defendants 1 and 2 were the previous registered owners of the disputed immovable property, which according to them constitutes a factory, but allege that Defendant 3 has no connection whatsoever with the issues in dispute and/or the disputed immovable property since she did not participate in or have any connection with the lending which the mortgage on the disputed immovable property secured, on the basis of which the transfer of its registration in the name of the Claimant was effected.





9. The Defendants allege that the transfer of registration of the disputed immovable property in the name of the Claimant was made by virtue of Mortgage ΥΧΧΧ dated 26/2/2010 for the sum of €264.000, plus interest, which was granted on the part of Defendants 1 and 2 for the further securing of the Loan of a third person dated 11/2/2010 from the Bank for the sum of €120.000. The latter terminated the said loan agreement on 24/9/2018 on the basis of alleged unpaid arrears and filed Action [number of previous action]. The aforementioned action was withdrawn and dismissed on 15/1/24 with costs in favour of the Defendants which will be payable in the event that the Claimant files an action in relation to the same the issues in dispute against any of the Defendants, something which the Claimant has not done.





10. The only letter of the Claimant according to the Defendants, was received by Defendant 3 and was the pre-action protocol dated 3/4/24. They allege however that its content does not comply with the procedure which the Civil Procedure Rules prescribe.





THE REPLY OF THE CLAIMANT





11. The Claimant filed a many-paged reply to the defence of the Defendants, through which it replies to the preliminary objections and allegations which are contained in the latter.





12. The Claimant, among others, maintains its positions as they emerge from the Statement of Claim, which to some degree it repeats, refers to the subject matter, the procedure and the circumstances which surrounded Actions No.[number of previous action] and No.[number of pending parallel action] alleging that the issues in dispute and purposes of these are not connected with the subject matter of the present one and consequently it has no obligation to pay any legal costs nor is the present procedure abusive.





13. It alleges that Defendant 3 is an existing person, mother of Defendants 1 and 2 and registered owner of a dwelling, in which they cohabit. The said dwelling adjoins the disputed immovable property, and the Defendants use the latter as a yard and garden and have unlawfully built a swimming pool which they also unlawfully use. The Defendant received the letter of the Claimant dated 5/2/24, on 8/2/24 and on behalf of Defendants 1 and 2, as mother and cohabitant.


III. APPLICATION UNDER CONSIDERATION & OPPOSITION


APPLICATION


14. There followed on 14/11/2024 the filing on the part of the Claimant of the present application, which is supported by an Affidavit of the property management officer at the Property Management Department of the Claimant (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-ΝΤ") and is founded, among others, upon Part 24 rules 1-6.


15. The deponent within the framework of the ΕΔ-ΝΤ repeats to a more extensive degree the pleaded positions of the Claimant, attaching relevant documents as exhibits in support of these and requests on behalf of the Claimant the issue of summary judgment against the Defendants. He states further that the Claimant believes that the Defendants have no real prospect of success of their defence and that there is no reason whatsoever, much less a compelling one, for which the case ought to be heard at a trial.


OPPOSITION


16. The Defendants filed a joint opposition, through which they advance various grounds for which the application of the Claimant ought not to succeed and which I set out summarily as follows: 1. The Application is legally and substantively unfounded and abusive and is pursued as a lever of pressure against the Defendants, 2. The preconditions for the issue of summary judgment do not exist and it is not justified by the facts of the ΕΔ-ΝΤ, 3. The Defendants have a very good defence and through their allegations triable issues emerge for which they must be given the right to present their version in support of their defence, 4. The Claimant conceals material facts and attempts to mislead, and 5. The filing of the action and of the application under consideration is judged to be malicious, vindictive and abusive which is contrary to what was agreed between the parties.


17. The facts upon which the opposition is based are set out in a joint affidavit of Defendants 1 and 2 (hereinafter the "ΕΔ of Defendants 1 and 2) and in a separate affidavit of Κ.ΜΑ, mother of Defendants 1 and 2 (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-ΚΜ).


18. Through the ΕΔ of Defendants 1 and 2, the deponents generally deny the positions and the evidence which was adduced in support of the Application of the Claimant, they allege that their mother is not named Κ.Μ. but Κ.ΜΑ and in any event has no connection whatsoever with the facts in dispute and the disputed immovable property. They deny the allegations of unlawful use of the disputed immovable property as a yard and garden and of unlawful construction of a swimming pool and of the use of it. They maintain that the disputed immovable property is a plot unconnected with the house where they reside with their family and into which the Claimant entered unlawfully during 2023 and a relevant complaint was submitted on their part to the Police. They also state that they received no letter whatsoever from the Claimant and allege that the letter of the Claimant dated 30/4/24 does not comply with the relevant Rules and does not serve the purpose of the extra-judicial procedure.


19. Defendants 1 and 2 admit that they were the lawful registered owners of the disputed immovable property which was mortgaged in favour of the Bank by virtue of mortgage ΥΧΧΧ of the District Land Registry Office dated 26/2/2010 for the sum of €264.000 plus interest until discharge. The mortgage was granted for the further securing of the Loan of a third person from [name of bank] dated 11/2/2010 for the sum of €120.000. [name of bank] terminated on or about 24/9/2018 the Loan Agreement on account of alleged unpaid arrears of the account and there was subsequently filed Action [number of previous action], which was withdrawn and dismissed on 15/1/2024 with costs in favour of the Defendants, which were payable in the event that the Claimants filed a new action in relation to the same issues in dispute against any of the Defendants. Their position is that the present action is about the same issues in dispute and about the same disputed immovable property and, since the Claimants did not pay the legal costs in Action [number of previous action], it is being promoted abusively.


20. In addition, the Defendants reply to the content of the ΕΔ-ΝΤ, and more particularly to the positions of the Claimant in relation to the circumstances under which Action [number of previous action] was withdrawn and they repeat their allegations of nullity of the procedure of transfer of registration of the disputed immovable property in the name of the Claimant.


21. The deponent in the ΕΔ-ΚΜ likewise proceeds to a general denial of the positions of the Claimant as they emerge from the evidence which was adduced through the ΕΔ-ΝΤ and more particularly of the references to unlawful trespass on her part on the disputed immovable property. She states that she is not Κ.Μ. but is named Κ.ΜΑ. and has no connection whatsoever with the facts in dispute and that she resides in a dwelling which in no case is property of the Claimant.
