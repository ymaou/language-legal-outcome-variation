1. The Claimants / Applicants by their Claim Form claim a declaratory judgment that the Defendant is trespassing unlawfully on the apartment (hereinafter the "Apartment"), an order for the delivery of free possession of the Apartment, an order which prohibits the Defendant from trespassing on the Apartment, judgment for rents in arrears and judgment in relation to intermediate benefits from 01.06.2024 up to and including the delivery of free possession of the Apartment.





2. The Defendant / Respondent in his Defence raises a preliminary objection in relation to the competence of the present Court to try the case and alleges that the Court competent by subject matter is the Rent Control Court.





3. He admits that he rented the Apartment from Claimant 1 by virtue of a tenancy agreement dated 22.08.2016 (hereinafter the "Tenancy Agreement") and that the tenancy expired one year later. He advances the allegation that the tenancy was renewed following an oral agreement with Claimant 1 from year to year. He admits that the rent during the year 2021 was fixed at €500 monthly. Further, he admits that he received the letter of termination 26.04.2024 and that he owed rents of two months which he had paid on 18.06.2024. He denies that he owes one rent and advances the allegation that there was natural wear which he repaired at his own expense.








B. APPLICATION AND EVIDENCE OF THE APPLICANTS





4. On 25.02.2025 a Reply to the Defence was filed. On the same day the Application was filed by which summary judgment is sought against the Defendant as per the claim inasmuch as the Defendant lacks a (good) defence and/or his defence has no real prospect of success.







6. The Application is supported by the affidavit of Claimant 1 (hereinafter the "ΕΔ ΣΣ") in which he states among others that:





6.1 The Apartment was erected above the ground-floor dwelling of his father. A share in the plot was transferred to him and subsequently he himself transferred it to his daughter (Claimant 2), however he retains a right of usufruct over it. He presents to that end Exhibits 1 - 3 ΕΔ ΣΣ.





6.2 From the time of the erection of the Apartment, namely from 2000 until 2006, he himself resided in it with his family. The Apartment was rented for the first time on 15.09.2006 and he presents the Tenancy Document as Exhibit 4 ΕΔ ΣΣ.





6.3 The Apartment was rented to the Defendant on 01.09.2016 until 31.08.2017 by virtue of the Tenancy Agreement - Exhibit 5 ΕΔ ΣΣ.





6.4 It constitutes an express term of the Tenancy Agreement that the tenancy period is not renewed automatically. There existed the possibility of renewal for one further year provided that the Tenant sent a registered letter to the owner two months before the expiry. And the rent would be paid monthly.





6.5 The Defendant did not send a letter for renewal by virtue of the Tenancy Agreement, however he remained in the Apartment. The tenancy was converted into one from month to month.





6.6 In 2021 by oral agreement the rent was fixed at €500 monthly.





6.7 On 04.01.2024 he sent a warning letter by registered post by which he called upon him to deliver the possession of the Apartment by 31.03.2024, providing that is to say reasonable notice to the Defendant of almost three months because Claimant 2 needed and needs the Apartment for purposes of owner-occupation ( Exhibit 6 ΕΔ ΣΣ). The Defendant did not reply to the letter.





6.8 Since the Defendant was not responding he turned to an advocate. His advocates upon his instructions sent a letter dated 26.04.2024 which was served on the Defendant on 29.04.2024 and by which the tenancy was terminated on 31.05.2024 and the Defendant was called upon to pay two rents in arrears ( Exhibit 7 ΕΔ ΣΣ).





6.9 On 01.06.2024 Claimant 2 visited the Apartment in order to take possession of it but the Defendant refused to deliver it. From 01.06.2024 the Defendant became an unlawful trespasser.





6.10 On 18.06.2024 the Defendant paid by way of remittance the sum of €1000 for two rents in arrears (March and April 2024) which the Claimant received with full reservation of his right to demand his eviction.





6.11 On 28.06.2024 a letter of demand on the basis of the pre-action protocol was sent and was served on 04.07.2024 ( Exhibit 8 ΕΔ ΣΣ). The Defendant did not reply to the letter.





6.12 The Defendant owes the rent of May (€500). On the basis of the market rental value of the Apartment the compensation for unlawful possession amounts to €1.100.





6.13 The Defendant has no good defence and abusively seeks the delay of the delivery of possession. He possesses the Apartment unlawfully, given that he has no right of possession and use of it.





7. In his Supplementary Affidavit Claimant 1 rejects the allegation that supposedly the Defendant was not notified of the issues at stake before the letter dated 28.06.2025. He had sent him a registered letter as well as a letter by process server. He received both letters ( Exhibits 6 and 7 ΕΔ ΣΣ).


8. He also rejected the allegation that the tenancy was renewed by oral agreement from year to year. He never agreed to such a tenancy. He adds that although any reason is not needed for the termination of the tenancy from month to month, he terminated the tenancy because the Defendant owed the rents.





9. There was also filed the affidavit of [name of valuer] who is a valuer of immovable property at the company [name of valuers' firm]. To his affidavit there is attached as Exhibit 4 - ΕΔ ΚΡ the valuation report dated 17.07.2025 which he himself prepared. According to the valuation the market rent amounts to €1.120 per month.








C. OPPOSITION AND EVIDENCE OF THE RESPONDENT





10. The Defendant submitted an opposition in the body of which eleven grounds of opposition are set out (hereinafter the "Opposition").





11. Among others it is advanced in the Opposition that the Defendant has a very good, substantial, plausible, arguable and bona fide defence and this emerges from his affidavit, he disputes the truth of the allegations of Claimant 1, he alleges that there are issues which must be resolved at a trial, the jurisdiction of the Court is disputed, he advances that the preconditions for the issue of summary judgment are not satisfied and the application is abusive and aims at the restriction of the right of the Defendant to a fair trial.





12. The Opposition is supported by the affidavit of the Defendant (hereinafter the "ΕΔ ΒΘ") in which there are stated, among others, the following:





11.1 He rented the Apartment for the first time on 22.08.2016 on the basis of the Tenancy Agreement ( Exhibit 1 ΕΔ ΒΘ).





11.2 He was never put on notice in any way before the letter 28.06.2024 which was served on him on 04.07.2025 (he refers to Exhibit 7 ΕΔ ΣΣ).





11.3 He denies the alleged owner-occupation of Claimant 2 and considers that any taking of fruits by Claimant 2 is not possible.





11.4 He has a good defence while the facts to which the Applicants refer are misleading.








11.5 As Exhibit 2 ΕΔ ΒΘ he attaches a document of the characteristics of the immovable property which he secured through the internet portal of the Land Registry in which it is stated that the Apartment was erected in 1998. This shows that jurisdiction lies with the Rent Control Court.





11.6 It is in the interest of Justice and is consistent with Article 30 of the Constitution that the case proceed to trial.
