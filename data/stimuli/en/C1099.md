I. INTRODUCTION



1. By the present Application, the Defendant / Applicant (hereinafter it will be referred to as the "Defendant") seeks orders by which a) the service of the Claim Form and Statement of Claim be set aside and/or annulled, b) the Claim Form and/or Statement of Claim be struck out and/or set aside and/or annulled and/or dismissed and c) summary judgment be issued for the dismissal and/or setting aside and/or striking out of the claim of the Claimant / Respondent (hereinafter the "Claimant").





II. THE CLAIM OF THE CLAIMANT





2. Through its claim form, the Claimant as the allegedly lawful and elected Management Committee of a jointly owned building (hereinafter the "Disputed Apartment Building"), which includes immovable property owned by the Defendant (hereinafter the "Disputed Unit"), claims from the latter, among others, the following: a) a sum in the amount of €696,52 as the participation and/or contribution of the Defendant in the expenses of maintenance, repair, restoration and management of the disputed apartment building (hereinafter the "Common Expenses") for the period 1/6/23 to 1/5/24 (hereinafter the "Disputed Period and b) a sum in the amount of €40,30 monthly as budgeted common expenses from 1/6/24 until the issue of judgment.



3. In the statement of claim which accompanies the Claim Form of the Claimant, allegations are advanced, among others, in relation to the following: a) the capacity of the Claimant and its election by the General Assembly of the owners of the apartments of the disputed apartment building, b) the capacity of the Defendant, as a company and as owner and/or entitled to registration of the disputed unit which forms part of the disputed apartment building, c) that during the disputed period, the share of the Defendant in the common expenses for the disputed unit in accordance with the Law and Standard Regulations and/or the General Agreement amounted to a sum in the amount of €696,52, d) that from 1/6/24 the share of the Defendant in the common expenses for the disputed unit in accordance with the Law and Standard Regulations and/or the General Agreement, amounts to the sum of €40,30 monthly, e) that the Defendant was called upon by the Claimant to pay the sums claimed by the present action, before its filing, which however it did not do, and f) that the Claimant has paid sums on behalf of and/or for the benefit of the Defendant, which has been unjustly enriched, and the Claimant is entitled to be compensated and/or restored.





III. APPLICATION UNDER CONSIDERATION & OPPOSITION


APPLICATION





4. The Application is supported by a many-paged Affidavit (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-ΝΜ") to which is attached a mass (24) of Exhibits. The deponent in the ΕΔ-ΝΜ states that he is a representative, employee and attorney of the Defendant. He acknowledges that the Defendant is the registered owner of the disputed unit and that the latter forms part of the disputed apartment building. On the basis of what he sets out, the Defendant disputes the capacity of the Claimant, as a lawfully constituted Management Committee for the disputed apartment building, in view of the fact that the latter was never registered as a jointly owned building in the competent land register and the deponent proceeds to set out the history and the facts facts which, according to him, led to the said finding on the part of the Defendant. As a result, the Defendant considers that the Claimant constitutes a non-existent person which consequently cannot claim or collect sums from the Defendant and lacks the capacity to take legal measures against it. In addition, the deponent states that the Defendant has itself made the necessary application for the registration of the disputed apartment building as jointly owned in the competent land register.





OPPOSITION


5. The Claimant filed an opposition, through which it advances various grounds for which the application of the Defendant ought not to succeed and which I set out summarily as follows: 1. The Application is an abuse of the procedure and aims at the delay of it, 2. It is not demonstrated that the action of the Claimant lacks a legal or factual foundation or anything which would justify the issue of the orders sought, 3. The Statement of Claim presents satisfactory and/or sufficient facts which demonstrate a good cause of action against the Defendant, 4. The matters which are raised are irrelevant to the Claimant and its claims, 5. The application is legally and substantively unfounded, 6. The ΕΔ-ΝΜ is insufficient and indefinite and does not provide a factual background for the Court to exercise its Discretion for the approval of the application, 7. The Application is made in bad faith and for ulterior purposes, is based on afterthoughts and aims at the misleading of the Court 8. The service of the action is lawful and its annulment will cause irreparable damage to the Claimant, and 9. The application is submitted late and without the Defendant having reserved its rights upon receipt of the action.





6. The facts upon which the opposition is based are set out in the attached affidavit of the president of the Claimant (hereinafter the "ΕΔ-ΣΑ), to which various exhibits (8) are attached, in support of what he states. In the ΕΔ-ΣΑ, in support of the grounds of opposition, allegations are advanced which answer the content of the ΕΔ-ΝΜ and which concern the lawfulness, according to the Claimant, of its constitution and operation.





7. In summary, the deponent states that a number of general assemblies of the owners of the disputed apartment building have been held, to which the Defendant was also invited but never participated. He states that the Claimant was elected at the general assembly dated 27/7/22 following the existence of the relevant quorum and since then its term is renewed annually since no owner raised an issue of termination of its term or expressed a wish to take part. Responsible for the collection of common expense debts from the owners was a private management company, which informed the owners of their debts and of the manner of payment of them. According to the deponent, the Defendant refuses to pay its common expense debts vindictively and for ulterior purposes and continuously creates obstacles and impediments to the orderly and smooth operation of the disputed apartment building.





8. In relation to the non-registration of the disputed apartment building as jointly owned, it emerges as the position of the Claimant that, it does not render it non-existent since such registration is not necessary in accordance with the Law for its election or its appointment, and, it does not negate the obligation of the Defendant to pay common expenses. The deponent states also that the Defendant never informed the Claimant of its intention to submit an application for the registration of the disputed apartment building as a jointly owned building and the Defendant proceeded unilaterally with the filing of it considering that it would overturn the Claimant.
